About

From the firehouse, to the pit, to your operation.

Station House Systems grew out of Station House BBQ — a real BBQ and catering company in Tampa, Florida. The playbooks on this site aren't theory. They're the systems we had to build to survive our own volume.

The operator behind it

Station House was founded and is operated by Anthony Fonseca — a firefighter who brought the firehouse operating discipline into a BBQ kitchen: drilled procedures, checklists, clear roles, and calm execution under pressure. When the bell rings, nobody improvises.

That background matters, because a Saturday rush and a big catering week behave a lot like an emergency scene: the outcome is decided by the systems you built before it started.

Why the systems exist

Station House had to sell events, smoke at volume, hold and finish product without losing quality, pack and deliver on time, staff it all, and keep the numbers straight — at the same time, every week. Smokers, combi ovens, holding, reheating, packing lines, delivery routes, sales pipelines, and large-event coordination: the systems exist because the company needed a repeatable way to execute under real pressure.

The BBQ Solutions Playbook is those systems, written down and given away — because most operators don't need more hype. They need the frameworks that survive contact with an actual kitchen.
